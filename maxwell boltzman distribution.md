# MAXWELL's BOLTZMAN DISTRIBUTION



The distribution describes the probalility of speeds for energy of gas particles at particular temperature.

T1>T2>T3 for the distribution graph . Where T1 has the greatest amplitudeand lowest velocity followed by T2 

then T3 with lowest amplitude and highest velocity.T1 is the most probable velocity(cp or vmp)



# **significance of the distribution to chemical kinetics**

***1; Activation energy -the curve helps to visualize the fraction of molecules with enough energy for a successful reaction.***

***2;reaction rate -the higher the temperature the higher the amount of particles proportion to exceed activation energy thus increasing the activation energy.***



***Distribution then allows for calculations of the speeds associated with the molecules*** 



***a) Most Probable velocity (MPV)***

***-this is the velocity possessed by the maximum number of molecules in a gas at a given temperature.***

***it corresponds to the peak of the curve.***

***its value of a given temperature depends on the volume of the gas .***



#### ***CALCULATIONS***



***Vmp={2Rt\\M}^1\\2***

***=1.41{Rt\\M}^1\\2***

&#x20;***It is the lowest of the 3 speeds***

***Vmp<Vavg<Vrms***



***where*** 

***R=universal gas constant***

***T=temperature in kelvins***

***M=molar mass in kilograms***





***b)Avarage velocity(Vavg)***



***the total displacement (change in position ) divided by the total time interval.***

***It represents the rate at which an object changes its position in a specific directions.***



***change in X\\Change in t***



#### ***CALCULATIONS***



***Vavg=1.59{Rt\\M}***



***c)Root mean square(Vrms)***



***the measure of speed particles of a gas .***

***Its defined as the squareroot of the average of the squared velocities of the molecules.***



#### ***CALCULATIONS***



***Vrms=1.73{RT\\M}^1\\2***
